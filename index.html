Diabetic Retinopathy Classifier

Diabetic Retinopathy Risks

Diabetic retinopathy is a complication of diabetes that affects the eyes. It can cause blindness if untreated.

Risk factors include high blood sugar levels, hypertension, and prolonged diabetes.

Classes and Causes

  • Class 0 (No DR): No signs of diabetic retinopathy.
  • Class 1 (Mild DR): Minor blood vessel swelling caused by high blood sugar levels.
  • Class 2 (Moderate DR): Blood vessels in the retina become blocked, impacting vision.
  • Class 3 (Severe DR): Extensive blockage in retinal blood vessels, leading to new abnormal vessel growth.
  • Class 4 (Proliferative DR): Advanced stage with fragile, abnormal blood vessels, causing potential vision loss.
